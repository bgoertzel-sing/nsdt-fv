## (iii) Replacing ToyEq by a Lean backend: no change to the trainer or the evaluator

- **Stub Lean backend:** `backends/lean_stub.py` (StubLeanBackend). It implements the A.3 CheckerBackend protocol
  with canned verdicts: an explicit verdict table first, then R8's rules on the text (sorry/admit, a new axiom, a
  changed statement), then Lean-token equality with the ground-truth proof. Snapshots are `<repo>@<window>`, and
  obligations are Appendix B records validated by `data/schemas.validate_obligation`. No Lean is installed or called.
- **Run:** `gate/g0_stub_run.py` trains B2 (`train/lora.Sequential`) and B6 (`train/joint.Joint`) stage by stage
  with `train/common.train_steps`. It samples with `train/model.sample`, checks every sample through
  `eval/runner.evaluate` with `CachedChecker`/`VerdictCache` over the stub, and computes the accuracy matrix and
  LA/FWD/ACC/F/BWT with `eval/metrics`. Every trainer and evaluator module is imported unmodified.
- **No change:** `git diff e876afe 771956a -- nsdt_fv/train nsdt_fv/eval` and `git status` on those folders are
  empty: 0 bytes, `TRAINER_EVALUATOR_DIFF_EMPTY=yes` (`runs/G0/diff_trainer_evaluator.txt`). The only S5 change
  outside new files is the constant `BENCHMARK_FIELD_MAP` in `data/schemas.py`. No validator changed.
- **No ToyEq:** the run executes in a fresh interpreter. It records `sys.modules`, and no ToyEq module, stream builder,
  ToyEq hold-out guard or mock backend was loaded before or after the run (`toy_modules_after: []` in
  `runs/G0/stub_run/result.json`). The test file, the driver and the stub contain no such import (AST check).
- **Test:** `tests/test_G0_stub_lean.py`, 7 passed. It covers the import checks (3), the stub's verdict rules, the
  no-ToyEq run, the run through the trainer and evaluator, and the canaries.

Plumbing numbers from the stub run (`runs/G0/stub_run/`; one seed, 12 test obligations, no intervals). These show

Canaries through the same `evaluate()` and metrics path: ground-truth proofs give ACC 1.0, F 0.0, all `ok`. "by sorry"
gives ACC 0.0, all `sorry`. A test obligation injected as a training example is refused by the guard.

**What "trainer" and "evaluator" cover here.** The evaluator is `eval/runner.py`, `eval/metrics.py` and
`eval/bootstrap.py`. The trainer is `train/` (B2, B5-p, B6, the shared loop, the model and sampler). `dt/dt_ref.py`
(DT-ref) imports only `train/model` and is unchanged (`git diff e876afe HEAD -- nsdt_fv/dt` is empty), but the stub
run does not exercise it. `eval/` also holds the experiment drivers `s0.py`-`s4.py` and their report generators. Those
drivers build the ToyEq stream and guard (`eval/s2.py` imports `data/holdout`, `data/stream` and, in one function,
`backends/toyeq`), so Stage C needs its own drivers. They are unchanged, and none of them is used by the stub run.

**Limit (ISSUES 51):** the hold-out guard (`data/holdout.py`) and the stream builder (`data/stream.py`) are
ToyEq-specific. They are data-side components, not the trainer or the evaluator, and the trainer takes the guard as a
callable. The stub run therefore uses a minimal identifier, statement and proof-text guard. A Lean guard and a Lean
stream builder are needed in C1. The tiny model's tokenizer is ASCII-only (ISSUES 52), so the stub stream is ASCII
Lean.

## (iv) What Stage S showed and what it did not

Shown:
- The harness works as specified. Evaluation sets are identical across arms (T4), and the hold-out guard raised 0 times
  in training while every injected canary raised (S1-S3). S2's numbers regenerate from the verdict logs, which is S2's
  harness-validation criterion.
- The toy has both phenomena under study: a learning gap of 28.4 points and forgetting of 11.9 (section ii).
- The symbolic channels emit checker-verified material. For example, channel B emitted 1,200 statements, all with
  accepted proofs (T10).
- The PLN reference fragment is correct on hand-computed cases (T11). On the toy property tables it ranks better than
  shape frequency, but only slightly: AUC 0.887 vs 0.847, +0.040 [0.032, 0.048]. Precision at k is +2-3% relative,
  far below G4's 20%. Brier is better (0.124 vs 0.139) and ECE worse (0.077 vs 0.054) (S4.md).

Not shown:
- **DT-ref learned nothing non-trivial on the toy (S2, S3).** No D arm solved a non-trivial test obligation (0 of 193;
  B5-10 39, B6 70). D1's ACC is 19.8 against B0's 17.8. Conjecture sleep produced 0 parseable new statements in 8,500
  generations. The kernel filter's effect could not be measured: D1 - D2 dACC is 1.1 with an interval that includes 0,
  although D2 trained on about 92% wrong proofs. Channels A-E on top of D1 changed nothing (all dACC about 0). The only
  DT-side finding is narrow: at equal, near-zero learning, re-derivation sleep keeps trivial competence that the
  cap-only B2c forgets (F 0 vs 9.0). **The DT questions therefore move to Stage C** (C4/C5, gate G3). The toy is
  calibrated for the baselines, and a cap on a frozen 0.8M-parameter base is too weak to test DT here.
- PLN's margin over frequency on the toy says little about G4. The toy table has near-duplicate rows across projects
  (ISSUES 43).
- Nothing about Lean was measured. S5 read the benchmark files only. Build times and extractable obligation counts are
  for C0/C1.

## (v) Decision

**G0: pass.** The reasons, condition by condition:
1. All Stage S acceptance tests pass: T1-T4 and T8-T11 at run level in their S reports, and the whole suite of 80 unit
   tests on the S5 head.
2. The toy shows a learning gap of 28.4 points (>= 20) and forgetting of 11.9 (>= 10).
3. Swapping in a Lean-shaped backend needed no change to the trainer or the evaluator. The diff against S4's head is
   empty, and the stub backend runs through both in a run that loads no ToyEq code.

Read with these qualifications: forgetting meets its target on the point estimate only (interval [9.3, 18.0]; seed 1:
10.5). The Lean-side guard and stream builder do not exist yet (ISSUES 51). DT's own questions are open (section iv).
